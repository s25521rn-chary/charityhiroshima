Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park

A place of memory, prayer, and hope for a world without nuclear weapons.

The Peace Memorial Park is home to the Atomic Bomb Dome. Beyond the Dome, it gathers several historical sites, each one honouring the victims of the bombing and carrying a message of peace.

  • Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum It holds belongings of the people who died in the atomic bombing, photographs, and a film that shows how the events of that day unfolded.
  • Children's Peace Monument It carries a bell that children ring to honour the young victims of the bombing and to pray for a world free of nuclear weapons.
  • The Cenotaph Memorial Monument It holds the registry of the names of the victims, acting as a resting place for their souls.
  • The Flame of Peace It burns throughout the year, set within a small pool of water, and will only be extinguished when the last nuclear weapon is gone.

花The Flower Festival (Hana Matsuri)

The Hiroshima Flower Festival takes place during Golden Week — Japan's longest holiday — across three consecutive days, from 3rd to 5th May.

It is a major celebration in the Peace Park, with candle lighting at night and a great mountain-like structure built entirely of flowers. It is also a wonderful opportunity to watch Kagura for free, enjoy Hiroshima's street food, and take in many other performances.
