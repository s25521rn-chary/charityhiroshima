宮島 · Miyajima

Miyajima Island

The island of the floating torii gate.

Miyajima is one of the islands of Hiroshima that so many people love to visit, thanks to the elegant scenery that has made it famous.

There are many things to see here, but these are the spots I look forward to most whenever I go:

  1. The beautiful torii gate. I always wanted to visit Miyajima before I even came to Japan — all because of this gate.
  2. Mt. Misen. You can ride the cable car / ropeway up the mountain. It is a wonderful spot to take in a clear view of the entire island.
  3. The five-story pagoda. An elegant landmark rising above the shrine grounds.
  4. The aquarium. A gentle stop, especially lovely with family.
  5. The Itsukushima Shrine. Home to a stage where Noh theatre is performed — notably each April.

The island is also famous for its deer, oysters, ice cream, and momiji (maple) — they are everywhere you look.
