The Atomic Bomb Dome

A brief history of the day Hiroshima changed forever.

It was on 6th August 1945, at 8:15 a.m., when an atomic bomb was dropped on Hiroshima.

Before this, everything was normal and people were going about their daily business, expecting nothing out of the ordinary. Then, all they heard was an explosion, and all they saw was a heavy cloud. At first, people did not realise that it was a bomb — only later did the truth become clear.

After some time, the black rain began to fall. It claimed many lives, because it carried radiation.

The Atomic Bomb Dome had been a commercial exhibition hall before its destruction. It is the only building that survived, and it was left standing as a reminder of the impact of the atomic bomb.
